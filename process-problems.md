# Process Problems

## Overview

The current customer complaint process contains several areas that may contribute to delays, inconsistent handling, and reduced customer satisfaction.

## Problem 1: Manual Complaint Assignment

Complaints are manually assigned to departments. This may result in delays or incorrect routing of complaints.

## Problem 2: No Automatic Escalation

There is no automatic mechanism to alert a manager when a complaint remains unresolved beyond an expected timeframe.

## Problem 3: Limited Visibility

Managers may not have a clear and timely view of open, overdue, and resolved complaints.

## Problem 4: Repeated Requests for Information

Customers may be asked to provide information more than once if information is not properly recorded or shared between teams.

## Potential Business Impact

These issues may contribute to:

- Longer complaint resolution times
- Increased workload for customer-service staff
- Poor customer experience
- Difficulty monitoring team performance
- Increased number of unresolved complaints
