# Business Requirements

## Project

Customer Complaint Process Improvement

## Objective

Improve the customer complaint process by reducing delays, improving visibility, and creating a more consistent method for managing and resolving complaints.

## Functional Requirements

### FR-01 — Complaint Submission

The system should allow customers to submit complaints and provide the information required for investigation.

### FR-02 — Automatic Confirmation

The system should automatically confirm that a customer's complaint has been received.

### FR-03 — Complaint Assignment

The system should assign complaints to the appropriate department based on the complaint category.

### FR-04 — Complaint Tracking

Staff should be able to view and update the status of complaints throughout the resolution process.

### FR-05 — Escalation

The system should automatically alert the appropriate manager when a complaint remains unresolved beyond the defined timeframe.

### FR-06 — Complaint History

The system should maintain a record of complaint activity, including updates, actions taken, and resolution status.

### FR-07 — Management Visibility

Managers should be able to view open, overdue, and resolved complaints.

## Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-01 — Security

Complaint information should only be accessible to authorized users.

### NFR-02 — Usability

The complaint management process should be simple and easy for staff to use.

### NFR-03 — Availability

Authorized staff should be able to access complaint information when required.

### NFR-04 — Auditability

The system should maintain a record of important changes made to complaint records.

## Expected Benefits

- Faster complaint response times
- Reduced complaint resolution times
- Improved visibility for management
- More consistent complaint handling
- Improved customer satisfaction
- Better monitoring of operational performance
