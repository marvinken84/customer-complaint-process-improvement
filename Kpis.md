# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## Project

Customer Complaint Process Improvement

## Purpose

The following KPIs will be used to measure whether the proposed process improvements achieve the desired business outcomes.

> Note: The current values and targets below are simulated for this portfolio project and do not represent real company data.

| KPI | Current Simulated Value | Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average first response time | 18 hours | 6 hours or less |
| Average complaint resolution time | 4.8 days | 2 days or less |
| Complaints resolved within 48 hours | 42% | 80% or higher |
| Customer satisfaction | 68% | 85% or higher |
| Complaints unresolved after 7 days | 15% | 5% or less |

## Why These KPIs Matter

### First Response Time
Measures how quickly customers receive an initial response after submitting a complaint.

### Resolution Time
Measures the average time required to completely resolve a complaint.

### 48-Hour Resolution Rate
Measures the percentage of complaints resolved within 48 hours.

### Customer Satisfaction
Measures how satisfied customers are with the complaint-handling experience.

### Overdue Complaints
Measures the percentage of complaints that remain unresolved after seven days.

## Expected Outcome

The proposed process improvements should reduce response and resolution times, increase the percentage of complaints resolved within the target timeframe, reduce overdue complaints, and improve customer satisfaction.
