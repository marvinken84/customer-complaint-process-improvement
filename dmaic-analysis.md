# Six Sigma DMAIC Analysis

## Project

Customer Complaint Process Improvement

> Note: This is a simulated portfolio project. All company information and performance data are fictional and used for educational purposes.

## 1. Define

### Problem Statement

The simulated company is experiencing slow customer complaint response and resolution times. This may negatively affect customer satisfaction and increase the workload of customer-service teams.

### Project Objective

Improve the customer complaint process by reducing response and resolution times, improving process visibility, and increasing customer satisfaction.

---

## 2. Measure

The following simulated baseline measurements were established:

- Average first response time: 18 hours
- Average complaint resolution time: 4.8 days
- Complaints resolved within 48 hours: 42%
- Customer satisfaction: 68%
- Complaints unresolved after 7 days: 15%

These measurements provide a baseline against which future performance can be compared.

---

## 3. Analyze

Potential causes of process inefficiency include:

- Manual complaint assignment
- Lack of automatic escalation
- Limited visibility of complaint status
- Inconsistent handling between departments
- Repeated requests for customer information

These potential causes may contribute to longer resolution times and reduced customer satisfaction.

---

## 4. Improve

The proposed improvements include:

- Automatic complaint categorization
- Automatic assignment to the appropriate department
- Complaint status tracking
- Automatic escalation of overdue complaints
- Improved management visibility
- Standardized complaint-handling procedures
- Customer satisfaction measurement

These improvements are intended to reduce delays and create a more consistent complaint-resolution process.

---

## 5. Control

The improved process should be monitored using the defined KPIs.

Key controls include:

- Regular monitoring of response time
- Regular monitoring of resolution time
- Tracking overdue complaints
- Monitoring customer satisfaction
- Reviewing escalation rates
- Periodic process performance reviews

If performance falls below the agreed targets, management should investigate the cause and take corrective action.

## Conclusion

The DMAIC analysis provides a structured approach for identifying potential causes of poor complaint performance, implementing process improvements, and monitoring results over time.
